What makes it different

Financial context, not a generic scan.

Validate is designed to answer a business question: how can a combination of exposures affect a critical financial operation?

  • Scenarios for banks, credit unions, fintechs and payment platforms
  • Relationships between identity, transactions, APIs, third parties and fraud
  • Paths oriented to business impact—not only isolated CVEs
  • Evidence of the route, not just a severity label
  • FINATTACK threat and process context
