Initial Credit Union scenarios

Start narrow. Create visible value.

These scenarios are commercially understandable and relevant to advisory, fraud, risk and cybersecurity stakeholders.

Digital banking and API exposure

Authorization, business logic, sensitive workflows, account access and member-facing routes.

Account takeover path

Credential abuse, session weakness, device change, MFA pressure and post-login monetization route.

Call center and recovery abuse

Pretexting, recovery workflows, identity verification gaps and escalation paths.

Onboarding fraud

Weak KYC flow, synthetic identity signals, early account abuse and risky member journeys.

ACH and payment abuse

New payee, limits, transfer workflow, control gaps and suspicious transaction sequences.

Third-party exposure

Vendor dependencies, fintech integrations, authentication providers and data-flow risk.
