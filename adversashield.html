The structural gap

Cybersecurity today is fragmented.

Defensive teams monitor continuously, but offensive validation remains periodic. SOC and SIEM platforms generate alerts every day, while penetration testing usually happens once or twice a year. Remediation sits in the middle—often disconnected from both.

AdversaShield closes that loop. We help organizations move from disconnected alerts, annual pentest reports and slow remediation cycles to a governed system of continuous validated risk reduction.

The problem

Three disconnected worlds.

Companies detect risk, test risk and fix risk in separate workflows. AdversaShield turns those workflows into one continuous loop.

Defense is continuous but overwhelmed

SOC, SIEM, MDR and monitoring systems operate 24/7, but analysts face alert fatigue, false positives, duplicated signals and slow triage.

Offense is powerful but episodic

Penetration testing and red teaming validate what attackers can exploit, but they usually happen only once or twice per year and often end in static reports.

Remediation is the weakest link

Even after a finding is discovered, teams struggle to know which issues matter most, how to fix them, whether the fix worked, and what evidence proves closure.

Benchmark strategy

Measured targets across pentesting, SOC AI and remediation.

Clear metrics across pentesting, SOC operations and remediation verification.

We are designing AdversaShield around a benchmark-first strategy. The platform will be evaluated across three dimensions: AI pentesting efficacy, SOC-AI triage performance and remediation verification. Our benchmark suite uses known environments such as OWASP Juice Shop, OWASP Benchmark, PortSwigger Web Security Academy, Hack The Box Insane-style labs, MITRE CALDERA, MITRE ATT&CK Evaluations and Atomic Red Team. The goal is to prove measurable reductions in false positives, MTTR, critical false negatives, remediation time and delivery cost.
