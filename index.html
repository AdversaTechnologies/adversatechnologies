Why Credit Unions first

A focused entry point into financial resilience.

Credit Unions face modern fraud, identity abuse, third-party risk and digital banking exposure while often operating with smaller security teams than large banks. Adversa starts here because the pain is specific, urgent and commercially actionable.

Adversa is not another scanner, report factory or chatbot. It is a closed-loop resilience platform that connects offensive proof, remediation readiness, financial context and continuous monitoring.
