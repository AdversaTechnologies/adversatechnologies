The operating problem

Security teams do not need more findings. They need proof.

Scanners identify possible vulnerabilities. Pentests demonstrate some of them. Ticketing systems track remediation activity. None of these alone proves that a critical attack path was completely closed.

AdversaShield connects demonstrated exposure, controlled correction, automated re-attack and evidence of resolution in one financial-sector resilience platform.
