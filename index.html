Our approach

Not more alerts. Verified risk reduction.

Adversa Technologies builds systems that prove what is exploitable, what was fixed and what evidence remains.

Is this exposure real?

Connect alerts and surfaces to governed offensive validation.

Can it be exploited?

Prove reachability with evidence gates and safe execution policies.

What should be fixed first?

Prioritize by exploitability and business impact—not scanner volume.

Did the fix work?

Retest the relevant attack path and preserve before/after proof.

Can defense detect it?

Feed closure evidence back into SIEM and analyst workflows.

What proves reduction?

Package executive and technical evidence of verified outcomes.
