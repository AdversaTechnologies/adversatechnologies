Evidence, not assumption

A patch being applied does not prove the vulnerability is closed.

Remediate produces a reviewable sequence from the original observed behavior to the post-remediation state.

The platform does not claim that a ticket, pull request or deployment is equivalent to risk elimination. The relevant attack must be repeated and the result observed.

1. Original exploit evidence

Inputs, scope, target state and observed impact from the validated finding.

2. Remediation artefact

Exact code, configuration, IaC or compensating-control change with provenance.

3. Isolated test evidence

Build results, regression checks, security tests and environment identity.

4. Re-attack result

Observed post-remediation behavior for the relevant exploit path.

5. Approval and operational state

Customer decision, rollback plan and post-deployment observation status.

Designed for control

Automation where it creates leverage. Human authority where it matters.

AdversaShield Remediate generates and verifies remediations in controlled environments, with human approval before production deployment.

  • Tenant and environment isolation
  • Rollback planning before approval
  • No claim of universal or risk-free correction
  • Unknown states are preserved—not converted into false success
